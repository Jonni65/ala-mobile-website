2022–2025 GROUND EFFECT ERA DETAILS

The 2022–2025 technical rules introduced Venturi floor aerodynamics designed to maximize high-speed cornering grip while allowing cars to follow closely.

VENTURI UNDERFLOOR TUNNELS

Generates massive low-pressure downforce under the chassis floor, relying on ground effect to maintain cornering grip at high speeds.

DRS (DRAG REDUCTION SYSTEM)

Single rear-wing flap opening within designated DRS zones when tracking within 1 second of a rival car at the detection point.

STIFF SUSPENSION & PORPOISING

Requires stiff suspension setups to manage ride-height consistency, demanding precise kerb riding and smooth steering inputs.

18-INCH WHEELS & 20-CAR GRIDS

Low-profile tires mounted on 18-inch wheels with 20-car starting grids across all championship and quick-race modes.

2026 CHASSIS TECHNICAL DETAILS

The 2026 regulation overhaul fundamentally alters how cars generate downforce and manage electrical battery power during wheel-to-wheel battles.

ACTIVE AERO: Z-MODE & X-MODE

Replace DRS with dual active wings. Shift to High-Downforce (Z-Mode) through corners, then manually switch to Low-Drag (X-Mode) on straights to dump drag and boost top speed.

ERS SUPERCLIPPING MECHANIC

At speeds above 290 km/h, the MGU-K electrical deployment tapers off dynamically. Drivers must store electrical charge to trigger high-power manual override bursts when chasing rival cars.

NARROW CHASSIS & WEIGHT REDUCTION

Chassis width is reduced by 10cm and total vehicle mass is dropped, resulting in significantly higher cornering agility, shorter braking distances, and sharper direction changes.

22-CAR EXPANDED GRIDS

All 2026 race modes now feature 22 active cars on track simultaneously, creating denser traffic management, tighter pit stop windows, and intense restart sequences.
